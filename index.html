Gas compliance checklist

• Appliance must be permitted/approved for LPG and installed to its instructions.
• Each appliance point requires an accessible shut-off/isolation valve.
• A fixed LPG installation requires an accessible emergency shut-off valve; on some single-appliance layouts the isolation/emergency function can be combined where permitted.
• Concealed joints, valves and unions must remain accessible for inspection, maintenance and repair — provide an access/inspection opening where cabinetry would otherwise hide them.
• Above-ground LPG pipework must be identified; estimator allows for line labels at approximately 2 m intervals.
• Composite pipe is not the final connection to the appliance; use an approved final connection and system-specific fittings.
• Geyser flue/ventilation depends on appliance type and manufacturer instructions; indoor Type B units require correct flueing/ventilation, while Type D units are outdoor units.
• Installation must be inspected/leak-tested and a gas CoC issued by an authorised registered practitioner.
